FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions —
Skin Care Clinic in Noida

Everything you want to know about skin treatments in Noida, acne treatment, laser procedures, and hair loss treatments.

How much does skin treatment cost at your clinic? ▼
Costs vary depending on the treatment and your skin condition. Basic treatments like peels start lower, while advanced procedures like laser or PRP cost more. You’ll get exact pricing after consultation.
Will I see results after the first session? ▼
Some treatments give visible improvement after 1 session, but most skin concerns (like acne scars or pigmentation) need multiple sessions for proper results.
Is there any side effect or risk? ▼
Mild redness or sensitivity can happen for a short time, especially after lasers or peels. Serious side effects are rare when done by experienced professionals.
How long does one session take? ▼
Most sessions take between 30 to 60 minutes depending on the treatment.
Do I need to take leave after treatment? ▼
Not really. Most treatments have little to no downtime. You can usually continue your daily routine the same day or next day.
Is consultation free or paid? ▼
In most cases, consultation is paid, but sometimes clinics run offers where it’s adjusted in treatment cost. Best to confirm before booking.
How many sessions will I need? ▼
It depends on your concern:
  • Acne: 3–6 sessions
  • Pigmentation: 4–8 sessions
  • Laser hair removal: 6–8 sessions
Are results permanent? ▼
Some treatments give long-term results (like laser hair reduction), but maintenance sessions may be needed depending on your lifestyle and skin type.
Can I start treatment if I have sensitive skin? ▼
Yes, but treatment will be customized. Doctors usually start with milder options to avoid irritation.
How soon can I start after consultation? ▼
In most cases, you can start treatment the same day or within a few days after your consultation.
